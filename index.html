About

Trainer, Advisor & EXCELpreneur

Saad Zubair Vohra, AFA-MIPA, CFM-UK — known to his students simply as Sir Saad Zubair Vohra — is an accomplished independent trainer, facilitator, and passionate EXCELpreneur, with over 15 years of experience delivering high-impact training sessions — online, in classrooms, and in-house.

He specializes in business and tax consultancy, financial modeling, and Excel-based solutions, helping business leaders solve complex challenges through data-driven strategies and advanced technology.

As a Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT), Saad is globally recognized for his instructional excellence, having conducted Excel training for professionals across industries — from basic functionalities to advanced tools like PivotTables, dashboards, and financial modeling.

Renowned for his engaging, results-oriented teaching style, he serves as visiting faculty with several educational institutions and continues to lead transformative training programs focused on rapid learning and long-term retention.
